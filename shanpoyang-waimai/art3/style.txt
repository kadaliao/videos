A genuine contemporary Chinese ink wash painting (shuimo, xieyi freehand style) on clean warm off-white xuan rice paper with faint natural fibers (no stains, no tears). Expressive wet brushwork: graded washes from deep black to the palest grey, ink blooms, dry-brush strokes, splashed ink, mist rendered by leaving the paper empty. Loose confident strokes, NOT pen lines, no ruler lines, no window grids, no hatching. Strictly monochrome black ink; no color anywhere unless explicitly requested below. No text, no signature, no seal, no calligraphy, no frame, no border, no watermark. Museum-quality fine art.
