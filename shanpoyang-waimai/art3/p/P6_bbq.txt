Night. Along the far side of the road, a lively night market of barbecue street stalls: billowing smoke from charcoal grills rising in layered ink washes into the sky, strings of round paper lanterns as pale circles, diners as loose ink silhouettes at small low tables, awnings. An extremely wide horizontal handscroll panorama, NO people and NO vehicles. Generic city, no famous or recognizable landmarks. A road runs perfectly horizontally across the entire width of the picture, about three quarters of the way down, drawn as long flat wet strokes, continuous from the left edge to the right edge. Both the left and the right edges fade into empty paper mist so the panorama can blend into neighbouring scenes.
