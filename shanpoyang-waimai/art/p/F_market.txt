Vertical composition. Night. The wide glass front of a large supermarket glows in the dark — the glow is created by leaving the paper empty against deep ink surroundings; shelves and goods inside are suggested with a few loose strokes; the dark wet pavement in front reflects the light. On the left side of the frame a delivery rider in gamboge yellow rides past on his scooter, small, slightly blurred with motion. The upper third is dark ink night sky.
