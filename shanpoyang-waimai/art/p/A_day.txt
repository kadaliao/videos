Vertical composition. A modern city painted as a misty mountain landscape: groups of tall skyscrapers rise like steep peaks out of drifting cloud and fog, receding in layers of ever paler ink, the farthest ones barely visible. A single long empty road curves gently across the lower fifth of the picture, drawn with one or two sweeping wet strokes. No people, no vehicles. The upper half is almost entirely empty pale sky and mist.
