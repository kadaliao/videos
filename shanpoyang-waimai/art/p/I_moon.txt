Vertical composition. A huge full moon — a perfect circle of unpainted paper surrounded by a soft ink-wash halo — hangs above dense residential apartment blocks at night. Many windows are lit, left as tiny white paper rectangles, and in some of them tiny silhouettes of families eating dinner together can be seen. Far below, on an empty street, very small, a delivery rider in gamboge yellow has stopped his scooter and looks up at the moon and the lit windows.
