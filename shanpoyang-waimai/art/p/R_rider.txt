A single small figure isolated in the center of a completely blank, pure white sheet of xuan paper, nothing else in the picture, no ground, no shadow beyond a faint wash under the wheels: a food delivery rider riding an electric scooter, seen exactly from the side, moving towards the right. He wears a gamboge-yellow jacket and a gamboge-yellow helmet; a square gamboge-yellow insulated delivery box is strapped behind the seat. The scooter, his trousers and shoes are painted in black ink with loose expressive brushstrokes. The figure is painted with a few confident wet strokes, like a master's xieyi figure sketch. The figure occupies about 60% of the picture width. Plenty of empty white paper around it on all sides.
