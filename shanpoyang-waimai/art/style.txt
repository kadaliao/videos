A genuine contemporary Chinese ink wash painting (shuimo, xieyi freehand style), painted with a wet brush on clean warm off-white xuan rice paper with faint natural fibers (no stains, no tears, no water marks). Expressive brushwork: soft graded washes from deep black to the palest grey, wet-on-wet ink blooms, dry-brush strokes, splashed ink (pomo), mist rendered by leaving the paper empty. Buildings are suggested with loose confident brush strokes, NOT drawn with pen lines or rulers, no hatching, no outlines, no window grids — like a master's landscape scroll. Generous empty space (liubai). Strictly monochrome black ink: the ONLY color permitted anywhere is gamboge yellow (teng huang), and only on the delivery rider's jacket, helmet and delivery box, if a rider appears. No other color at all, no text, no signature, no seal, no calligraphy, no frame, no border, no watermark. Museum-quality fine art, poetic, quiet, restrained, melancholic.
